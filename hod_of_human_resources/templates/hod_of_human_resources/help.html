Frequently Asked Questions

To create a new job requisition:

  1. Navigate to HR Management → Job Requisitions
  2. Click "Create New Requisition"
  3. Fill in all required fields including job title, department, and requirements
  4. Submit for approval

Employee information can be updated through the HR Management module:

  1. Go to HR Management → Workforce
  2. Search for the employee
  3. Click "Edit" next to their profile
  4. Update the necessary information
  5. Save changes

Reports can be generated from the Analytics section:

  1. Navigate to Analytics
  2. Select the type of report you need
  3. Choose date range and filters
  4. Click "Generate Report"
  5. Download or view the report
